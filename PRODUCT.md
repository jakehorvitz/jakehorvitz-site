# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, founders, and collaborators in AI, music, and entertainment who land on jakehorvitz.com from LinkedIn, GitHub, or a cold email. They are deciding in under a minute whether Jake Horvitz is a real builder or a resume. Most arrive on a laptop in the evening; a meaningful share arrive on a phone from a LinkedIn tap.

## Product Purpose
A one-page portfolio of the AI projects Jake shipped solo with Claude Code: autonomous agents (Bartok, JARVIS, the Philosopher), the Horvitz pipeline, a voice assistant, and the operations tools behind live music, real estate, and campus politics. Success is a visitor who scrolls the whole page, opens at least one carousel, and emails or connects. Every project shown runs in production for a real user.

## Brand Personality
Operator, kinetic, unsentimental. The page should feel like the control room the agents live in, not a template about them. Three physical words: control room, stage light, ledger. Copy is first person, low ceremony, no hype adjectives, and every claim is one Jake can defend on a call.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI-startup landing pages: purple gradients, glass cards, gradient text, identical icon-heading-text card grids.
- Terminal cosplay: monospace everything, neon green on black, fake shell prompts with invented output.
- Editorial-magazine portfolio: italic display serif, drop caps, ruled columns, no imagery.
- Anything that fabricates numbers, screenshots, or agent output. Staging shots with fixture data are fine and are labelled as such.

## Design Principles
- Show the systems, not adjectives: real screenshots, real agent portraits, real stage rails; imagery carries the page.
- The agents are characters: each one gets a face, a rule it cannot break, and a blurb a stranger can repeat.
- One person, strict process: the page's own craft is evidence of the pipeline it describes.
- Motion is choreography, not decoration: one strong opener, purposeful reveals, carousels that feel physical.
- Drafts, not sends: nothing on the page implies an agent acts on its own.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text at or above 4.5:1 on its ground. Full keyboard operation for nav, carousels (arrow keys, buttons, dots), and links. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses the particle field to a static frame and all reveals to crossfades. Alt text describes what each screenshot shows. No content is gated behind hover.
